Our Programme

What We Deliver

Moulescoomb Boxing Academy runs two connected but separate programmes — and sponsorship helps power both, so no child is priced out of either.

In-School Programme

Free · During the school day

Free taster sessions, PE curriculum enrichment and after-school clubs bring boxing directly into schools across Brighton & Hove — introducing pupils to the sport at no cost to them or their school.

  • Free On-Site Taster Sessions
  • PE Curriculum Enrichment
  • After-School Boxing Clubs

The first step — free. Open to every pupil, at no cost to them or their school.

Free school sessions supported by

Sunday Academy Programme

At the University of Brighton, Falmer

Pupils who want to go further can progress onto our structured Sunday programme — following the 7-Level Progression Pathway from Foundation through to Mastery.

  • Foundation → Development → Confidence
  • Advancement → Leadership
  • Achievement → Mastery

This is a paid programme. Sponsorship can fund full places for children who couldn’t otherwise afford to take part.

Both programmes are delivered by Moulescoomb Boxing Academy — Sponsorship can support the school sessions, the Sunday Academy, or both. Partner With Us

Getting Started

How to Join

  1. 1

    Book a free taster

    Book a free taster session through Gymdesk. Free tasters run in our Level 1 session on Sundays, 10:00 – 11:00am. No experience is needed – just bring comfortable sportswear and a bottle of water.

    Need kit? Visit our shop for starter kits, hand wraps and grip socks.

  2. 2

    Enrol on Gymdesk

    If your child enjoys it, enrol through Gymdesk: a one-off £20 enrolment fee (including the Level 1 Academy T-shirt) and £45 per month for one structured Sunday session per week.

  3. 3

    Start at Level 1

    Your child starts at Level 1 (Foundation) in the white T-shirt and works up through the 7 levels, with optional assessments by invitation every 12–13 weeks.
