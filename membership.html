Membership Fees

Monthly Membership Fee

£45 per month

Membership fees are £45 per month and are collected via a recurring monthly subscription through Gymdesk.

The monthly membership fee covers one structured Academy training session per week.

One Time Enrolment Fee

£20

A one-off enrolment fee of £20 applies and includes the official Level 1 Academy T-shirt.

The enrolment fee and assessment fees are non-refundable.

Assessment Fee

£39 per assessment

Assessments typically take place at the end of each 12–13-week training block and are by invitation only. Participation is optional; successful assessment is required to progress to the next level.

Successful boxers receive their next-level T-shirt, Certificate of Achievement and written assessment report. Level 1 assessment also includes the Assessment & Achievement Handbook.

Assessment fees, additional club sessions, Academy kit, competitions, events and optional purchases are charged separately where applicable. Browse Academy kit in our shop.

Benefits for Your Child

Our 7-level boxing programme is designed to support young people both physically and mentally. Through structured, age-appropriate training, children can benefit from:

  • Improved confidence and self-esteem
  • Better focus, discipline, and listening skills
  • Positive use of energy in a safe, controlled environment
  • Improved coordination, balance, and overall fitness
  • Learning respect, resilience, and emotional regulation
  • A sense of achievement through clear goals and progression

No prior boxing experience is required, and all activities are adapted to suit the age and ability of each child.

Who Is This Programme Suitable For?

The programme is suitable for children who:

  • Enjoy physical activity or want to try something new
  • Would benefit from increased confidence or structure
  • Need support with focus, self-regulation, or wellbeing

Extra Sessions

Junior Club Sessions

Optional junior sessions are also available at Moulescoomb ABC on Wednesdays 5–6pm and Fridays 6–7pm for £5 PAYG.

These sessions are separate from Academy membership and are subject to the current club timetable and charges.

Private Sessions

Some of our coaches offer private 1:1 coaching sessions, subject to availability. These sessions are charged separately from membership. Please speak to Hayley or Nick for further information.

Missed Sessions, Pauses & Cancellations

Missed Sessions

Missed Academy sessions are non-refundable and cannot be carried forward, transferred, exchanged or credited unless otherwise agreed by the Academy.

Pausing Membership

Membership pauses are available for extended absences, such as holidays of two weeks or more. During an agreed pause period, membership payments are suspended and reactivated when your child returns to training. Please contact the Academy in advance to arrange this.

Cancelling Membership

Membership cancellations must be submitted in writing and are subject to 30 days’ notice. Any membership payments that fall due during the notice period remain payable. The enrolment fee, assessment fees and any membership fees already paid are non-refundable.

Parent Expectations

Parents and carers are expected to:

  • Encourage regular attendance and punctuality
  • Respect coaching decisions and progression timelines
  • Communicate concerns outside of training sessions
  • Support their child without applying pressure

Behaviour & Conduct

All boxers are expected to show respect, follow instructions, train safely, and represent the academy positively. Behaviour issues will be addressed constructively, with parents or carers informed where appropriate.
