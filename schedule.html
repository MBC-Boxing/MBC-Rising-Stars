Level Progression Overview

Our 7-Level Youth Development Programme supports boxers as they develop skill, confidence, and discipline over time. Each level is carefully structured to build specific abilities and is identified by a unique T-shirt colour. Assessments are conducted by invitation only when coaches feel a child is confident and ready to progress.

Programme focus: Stance, guard, footwork basics, jab and rear hand technique, & basic defences (block, duck, step off).

Assessment type: Boxing fundamentals, using coach and partner pads (no contact).

Level 2Development · Red T-shirt

Programme focus: Jab & rear hand multiple combinations, extended foot movement and defensive skills (covers and lay-back).

Programme focus: Introduction to lead hook (head), punch mechanics in 3-shot combos, and dynamic defences (slips and rolls).

Assessment Information

Assessments take place approximately every 12–13 weeks and are conducted by invitation only. Assessments take place during your child’s normal training session time. Invitations are given when coaches feel a child is technically ready, confident, and consistently showing effort and discipline.

Successful boxers will progress to the next level and receive their new level T-shirt, Certificate of Achievement and written assessment report. Level 1 assessment also includes the Assessment & Achievement Handbook.

Assessment fees are charged separately and are currently £39 per assessment.

Participation in assessments is optional. Boxers who choose not to take part may continue training at their current level and can be considered for a future assessment when ready.

What Happens in a Typical Session?

Each training session at Moulescoomb Boxing Academy is structured to balance fitness, skill-building, and confidence development:

  1. Warm-up: Light cardio, mobility drills, and dynamic stretches.
  2. Skill Development: Footwork drills, stance, guard, punches, and defensive techniques.
  3. Conditioning: Fun fitness circuits and activities to improve strength and stamina.
  4. Controlled Partner Work: Non-contact technical drills with a partner.
  5. Cool-down: Stretching and mental focus exercises to finish the session.

Note: We introduce contact sparring progressively once fundamental skills are established and the child is ready. See our Contact & Partner Work Policy for full details.
