What Sponsorship Supports

Moulescoomb Boxing Academy runs two connected but separate programmes — and sponsorship helps power both, so no child is priced out of either.

In-School Programme

Free · During the school day

Free taster sessions, PE curriculum enrichment and after-school clubs bring boxing directly into schools across Brighton & Hove — introducing pupils to the sport at no cost to them or their school.

The first step — free. Open to every pupil, at no cost to them or their school.

Sunday Academy Programme

Brighton University Sports & Health Complex

Pupils who want to go further can progress onto our structured Sunday programme — following the 7-Level Progression Pathway from Foundation through to Mastery.

This is a paid programme. Sponsorship can fund full places for children who couldn’t otherwise afford to take part.

Both programmes are delivered by Moulescoomb Boxing Academy — Sponsorship can support the school sessions, the Sunday Academy, or both.

The academy is actively expanding into more primary and secondary schools across the city — every new partner school reaches dozens of families and educators, all of whom will see your brand supporting their community.
