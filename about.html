About Us

Moulescoomb Boxing Academy is a youth-focused, community-rooted boxing programme dedicated to developing confident, disciplined, and skilled young athletes across Sussex. We proudly operate alongside Moulescoomb ABC, the competitive boxing club founded by Head Coach Nick Bell. Hayley Weller, who first competed for Moulescoomb ABC, went on to serve as club secretary, welfare officer and matchmaker, and has been instrumental in running our junior and women-only sessions, as well as supporting our competitive squad. Their shared commitment continues to shape both the academy and club into inclusive, high-performing environments for young people to thrive.

Our MissionOur mission is to develop confident, disciplined young people through structured boxing, promoting respect, resilience, and personal growth at every stage.

Our Story

Why We Do This

Moulescoomb Boxing Academy began with a simple belief: every child deserves the chance to build confidence, discipline and resilience — regardless of their background or postcode.

Too many young people across Brighton & Hove miss out on structured sport simply because their family can't afford it. We saw first-hand how boxing changes lives — not through fighting, but through focus, respect and hard-earned self-belief. So we made a decision: take boxing out of the gym and into schools, completely free of charge, and put it directly in front of the children who stand to gain the most.

That's why we do this, and why we need partners like you to help us reach every child who could benefit.

Why We Developed the Programme

Too often, children can get lost in traditional boxing gyms without structure, clear progression, or short-term goals. This can lead to young people feeling overlooked or losing motivation.

We created this structured, 7-level programme to give every child a clear path forward. Each stage helps build confidence, skill, and a sense of achievement — while also promoting personal development both inside and outside the ring.

Our 7-Level Pathway

Each child progresses through our carefully structured curriculum, starting with foundational skills and building towards competitive boxing or leadership roles. Each level emphasises personal growth, discipline, and achievement — fostering success inside and outside the ring.

  • Level 1Foundation
  • Level 2Development
  • Level 3Confidence
  • Level 4Advancement
  • Level 5Leadership
  • Level 6Achievement
  • Level 7Mastery

Boxing Background

Nick is a multiple-time national boxing champion with 68 competitive bouts under England Boxing. He began boxing at the age of 11 and has achieved the following:

  • Schools Championship Finalist
  • Junior ABA Champion
  • Junior NABGC Finalist
  • Senior NABGC Champion
  • Senior Elite Championship Finalist

With over 20 years of coaching experience, Nick brings a wealth of technical knowledge and a deep commitment to helping young people develop discipline, resilience, and confidence.

Boxing Background

Hayley began training and competing in kickboxing and Wing Chun at the age of 12, before becoming a highly accomplished competitive boxer in 2008 with 25 bouts under England Boxing. She is a:

  • 3x Southern Counties Champion
  • 2x National Development Finalist
  • Senior Elite National Championship Finalist
  • Haringey Box Cup Finalist
  • Current Head Coach for University of Brighton Panthers Boxing Team

With over 12 years of coaching experience, Hayley is passionate about helping young people grow through boxing — developing skills that foster resilience, confidence, and discipline far beyond the ring.

Inclusive coaching for neurodivergent children and adults with Down's syndrome DBS checked | Safeguarding & first aid trained

Together, Nick and Hayley lead the academy with a shared vision: to build champions inside and outside the ring.
